我现为上海交通大学三年级本科生，在 ScaleLab 师从穆尧（Yao (Mark) Mu）教授。此外，我目前在南加州大学（USC）的 Physical Superintelligence (PSI) Lab 做研究，导师为王岳教授。我的研究兴趣涵盖具身智能、视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型、Agent、人形机器人（Humanoid）、视觉-语言模型（VLM）以及面向机器人的 Agent Harness。

同时，我也非常喜欢长跑和烘焙，欢迎来吃我烤的可爱兔子饼干:)

I am currently a third-year undergraduate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working in ScaleLab under the supervision of Prof. Yao (Mark) Mu. I am also doing research in the Physical Superintelligence (PSI) Lab at the University of Southern California, under the supervision of Prof. Yue Wang. My research interests span embodied intelligence, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, agents, humanoid robots, Vision-Language Models (VLMs), and agent harnesses for robotics.

I also love long-distance running and baking, and you are welcome to come try the cute bunny cookies I bake :)
