I am a junior undergraduate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Yao (Mark) Mu. My research interests include embodied intelligence, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, agents, humanoid robots, Vision-Language Models (VLMs), and agent harnesses for robotics. I also love long-distance running and baking, and you are welcome to come try the cute bunny cookies I bake :)
